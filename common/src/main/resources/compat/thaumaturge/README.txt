Optional Thaumaturge aspects and crucible support only.
NeoForge data: data/thaumaturge/data_maps/item/base_aspects.json.
Thaumaturge reads those item aspects for scanning and for crucible dissolution.
Bamboo gives 4 Ignis and 1 Herba; a nine-bamboo bundle gives 36 Ignis and 9 Herba.
Block-only plant states and wall signs use their normal obtainable item equivalents.
The original blaze-rod recipes produce minecraft:blaze_rod, whose aspects belong to Thaumaturge.
No wand parts, research, or recipes are added by this integration.
